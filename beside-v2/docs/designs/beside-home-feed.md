# Design: Beside home feed, Hinge grammar with a Tesla look

## Problem Statement
The founder wants Beside's UI to feel like Hinge or Tinder ("swipeable"), to adopt the Tesla design language from `npx getdesign@latest add tesla`, to remove all green, and to make the buttons blue. Beside is a paid, platonic, one-to-one conversation-practice service in Indian cities. Men book verified companions for a session in a public venue, with masked chat, check-ins and an SOS button. A 30-route Next.js prototype exists, built on a Sage (green) palette with a horizontal photo rail.

The real question underneath is how to make browsing feel modern and familiar without making Beside read as a paid dating app.

## Demand Evidence
None yet. The stage is pre-product: a prototype with no members, no companions and no paid sessions. This doc changes the UI only. It does not create demand evidence, so the Assignment carries the demand test.

## Status Quo
Per the founder, the target men use dating apps (Hinge, Bumble, Tinder) partly to practise talking to women. That is the habit Beside competes with, and it is why the Hinge reference feels natural.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **User:** the founder, or a close friend, who has the problem first-hand. That is strong insight into the pain, but it is one person, so still a hypothesis.
- **Wedge (not yet tested):** one paid, in-person conversation session with one verified companion, brokered by hand before the app is needed.

## Constraints
- Two-sided marketplace. Companion supply is the harder side, and companions must feel safe being listed.
- The category is publicly framed in India as "rent-a-boyfriend" or "rent-a-romance", with no dedicated regulation, so anything that signals dating raises reputational, app-store and payment-provider risk.
- The existing safety and privacy guarantees stay intact:
  - logged-out viewers only ever load soft (pre-blurred) photo files;
  - masked chat with number and link guards;
  - SOS hold;
  - one primary action per screen;
  - 44px tap targets;
  - no horizontal scroll at 360px;
  - reduced-motion support.
- Banned in copy and UI, as before: rent, girlfriend, date, hookup, hearts, flames, swiping on people, match scores.
- The Tesla `DESIGN.md` from getdesign is unofficial and "inspired by", not Tesla's own system. Use it as a style reference only, with no Tesla names, logos or marks.

## Premises (agreed)
1. Pre-product, the bottleneck is trust on both sides, especially companions, not browsing speed. The redesign should look *more* trustworthy, not more like dating.
2. Borrow Hinge's visual grammar: full-bleed photos, one person per screen, respond to a specific part of a profile. Drop its judgment mechanics: swipe left/right on people, likes, X buttons, matches.
3. The Tesla-inspired `DESIGN.md` is a style reference only:
   - white canvas, near-black ink (`#171A20`), mid grey (`#5C5E62`), light grey (`#F4F4F4`);
   - full-viewport photography;
   - one action blue, the `--color-action` token, as the fill of every primary button;
   - every green or sage token removed.
4. The SOS red (`#B3402F`) stays as the one functional exception.
5. Demand comes before the feed. The palette swap (blue, no green) can ship now. The full-bleed feed is built only if the companion interviews in the Assignment pass the gate below.

## Build Gate
- **Now, regardless of interviews:** the palette and button swap (Approach A's scope). Every green or sage token goes, and blue `--color-action` primary buttons come in. This is what the founder asked for and is cheap.
- **After the 3 companion interviews:**
  - If at least 2 of 3 say they would list themselves in the full-bleed feed and not *less* willingly than in the rail, build Approach B.
  - If fewer than 2 say yes, or they prefer the rail, keep the reskinned rail and revisit Approach C once topic data exists.

## Approaches Considered
- **A. Reskin in place.** Tokens and buttons only. Rejected as the end state because it doesn't deliver the Hinge feel that was asked for. Its scope ships first, per the Build Gate.
- **C. Topic first, then a paged deck.** Strongest anti-dating signal. Deferred until the demand test shows which topics men actually pick.

## Recommended Approach: B, a full-bleed profile feed with Hinge grammar
Reference wireframe: `/Users/azim/besides/.gstack/tmp/beside-feed-wireframe.png` (copy at `~/.gstack/projects/besides/designs/mockup-20261029/feed-wireframe.png`).

**Feed mechanics**
- Home is a vertical list of companion items inside a `scroll-snap-type: y mandatory` container. Each item has `scroll-snap-align: start`.
- **Sizing (design review 6.2):**
  - the feed is a `position: fixed` scroll container (inset 0), constrained to the 390px frame on desktop with `max-height: 900px`;
  - each item is `height: 100%` of that container, not `100dvh`, so nothing resizes when the mobile toolbar shows or hides;
  - verify on a real iPhone (Safari) and a real Android phone (Chrome) before shipping.
- Vertical scrolling *only* moves between companions. Nothing inside an item scrolls, so the two scroll levels never compete.
- Tapping the left or right half of the photo cycles that companion's photos. Thin segmented bars at the top show the position.
- **At the ends (design review 2.2):**
  - cycling stops at the first and last photo, with no wrap-around;
  - a tap past either end does nothing, and the matching "Previous photo" or "Next photo" button is `disabled`;
  - a companion with one photo shows no bars and no tap zones.
- The rest of a profile ("Ask me about", "A good first session with me", "Where I meet", "Languages") lives on a separate full-screen detail view: the existing `/companions/[id]` route, restyled. It opens from the tappable name row ("Meera ›").
- **Accessibility:**
  - each item is an `<article>` with an `<h2>` name inside a list, so screen readers read companions in order;
  - **keyboard map (design review 6.1):**
    - Up/Down and Page Up/Page Down move between companions, and Home/End jump to the first and last;
    - Left/Right cycle the focused companion's photos, stopping at the ends per 2.2;
    - Tab order within an item: name row, then "Request about this", then the primary button;
  - photo cycling uses two real buttons ("Previous photo", "Next photo") that fill the tap halves.

**Navigation (design review 1.1)**
- A thin top bar sits over the photo on a short top gradient (black at 45% opacity fading to 0 over 96px, plus the safe-area inset).
- Left: the wordmark "Beside" in white.
- Right: a 44px "Sessions" icon button (verified), or a "Sign in" text action (logged out), which opens the gate in sign-in mode.
- No bottom tab bar, so nothing competes with the overlay's primary button.

**Overlay legibility (design review 4.1)**
- A fixed scrim covers the bottom 60% of each item: black at 0%, 35% and 78% opacity, at 0%, 40% and 100% of the scrim's height.
- Overlay text sits only in the bottom 40% of the item, where the scrim is at least 60% black.
- Acceptance check: white overlay text reaches at least 4.5:1 even over a pure white (#FFFFFF) photo.

**Item layout (design review 1.2: three blocks)**
Bottom overlay on that scrim, three blocks only:
1. **Who:** the name row "Meera ›" is one tappable target that opens the profile; it replaces the separate "More about Meera" action. Under it, one line: "Communication coach · Bandra · Verified · 4.8". **New companion (design review 2.1):** until her first rating exists, the line ends at "Verified" ("Communication coach · Bandra · Verified"), with no "0.0", no blank and no "New" tag.
2. **What she's good for:** one prompt card (white surface, "Ask me about: Speaking up in meetings", with the "Request about this" text action).
3. **Act:** the one primary button.

**Action hierarchy (one primary per screen)**
- **Primary:** the only element with a filled `--color-action` background is "Request a session" (verified) or "See full profiles" (logged out). There is exactly one per viewport.
- **Prompt card:** "Request about this" is a secondary text action, `--color-action` text on the white card surface, with no fill.
- **Name row:** "Meera ›" is white text with a chevron on the dark gradient, not a fill.
- No blue *fills* appear anywhere else.

**Profile detail view (design review 7.1)**
`/companions/[id]`, member-only:
- **Top 70% of the viewport:** the same full-bleed photo with its photo bars and tap halves (rules 2.2 and 6.1), plus the top bar with a 44px Back button in place of the wordmark.
- **Below, on white:** the name (32/36, weight 500), the stats line (13/18), then flat sections separated by 1px `#E6E6E6` hairlines, with no cards:
  - "Ask me about": each prompt with its own "Request about this";
  - "A good first session with me";
  - "Where I meet": public venues only;
  - "Languages".
- **Primary:** "Request a session" is sticky at the bottom with safe-area padding. It is the screen's one primary.
- **States:**
  - loading: a grey photo block and skeleton lines;
  - companion not found: "This profile isn't available", with a secondary "Back to companions";
  - image failure: per the feed rule.

**Logged-out intro item (design review 3.1)**
Logged out only, the first snap item is a full-viewport white screen with no photo:
- the top bar;
- the headline "Good conversation, in good company." (sans, see the type scale);
- the line "Public places. Verified people. Your pace.";
- the three-row "It is / Where / It isn't" block from today's welcome screen ("It isn't: anything romantic or physical. No private meetings, no exchanging numbers.");
- the primary "See companions", which scrolls to the first companion. On the intro, that is the one primary.

Companions start one scroll below. Verified members never see the intro.

**Logged out (every action defined)**
- Soft photo files only. Photo cycling works across the soft files.
- These all open the existing gate sheet: "See full profiles", "Request about this" and the name row. The profile detail view stays member-only.
- No action is hidden, so the layout matches the verified feed.

**Intent and restore**
- **What's stored:** before the gate opens, store `intent = { companionId, feedIndex, promptId | null, target: "feed" | "profile" | "plan" }`.
- **Which `target` (design review 7.2):**
  - the name row stores `"profile"`; after verification the user lands on `/companions/[id]`, and the soft-to-sharp crossfade plays there;
  - "Request about this" stores `"plan"`;
  - every other action stores `"feed"`.
- **Which value wins:** `companionId` is authoritative and `feedIndex` is only a hint. After verification, look up the companion's current position; fall back to `feedIndex` only if the id is in the list but the lookup fails.
- **With a prompt:** if `promptId` is set, go straight to `/plan` with that companion and the prompt's topic pre-filled in the note.
- **Without a prompt:** restore the feed scrolled to that companion. The sharp file mounts and the soft one crossfades out (opacity only, about 500ms, instant under reduced motion), exactly as `UnlockImage` does today.
- **Companion gone:** if the companion is no longer listed (removed, paused or filtered), open the feed at the top with a one-line notice, "Meera isn't available right now", and clear the intent.

**Reduced motion (design review 6.3)**
Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`:
- `scroll-behavior: auto` for snap scrolling, "Back to top" and the restore scroll;
- photo changes and the unlock crossfade are instant, with no fade;
- no skeleton pulse;
- only focus rings and instant state changes remain.

**Feed states**
- **Loading:** one full-viewport item with a grey photo block (opacity pulse only) and skeleton bars where the name, role and button go.
- **Empty:** full-viewport message, "No companions in your area yet", with "Join the waitlist" as the one primary.
- **Error:** full-viewport message, "We couldn't load companions", with a secondary "Try again". No primary.
- **End of feed:** a final snap item reading "You've seen everyone near you", with a secondary "Back to top".
- **Image failure (soft or sharp):** the photo area falls back to light grey `#F4F4F4` with the companion's initial in mid grey. The overlay gradient and text stay readable, and the sharp-unlock crossfade is skipped.

**Visual system**
- Replace the Sage tokens in `app/globals.css` with the Tesla-inspired palette.
- `--color-action` is the reconciled primary blue (see Dependencies), and white text on it must reach at least 4.5:1.
- Buttons become rectangular (4px radius):
  - **primary:** `--color-action` fill with white text, darker on hover, darker still when pressed, scale 0.98;
  - **secondary:** white with a 1px grey border;
  - **ghost and text actions:** `--color-action` text on light surfaces only.
- Sans-serif headlines throughout. Fraunces is dropped to match Tesla's single geometric sans; Plus Jakarta Sans stays unless the `DESIGN.md` names a closer free font.
- **Browser surfaces (design review 5.3):**
  - focus ring: 2px `--color-action` outline with a 2px offset, white over photos;
  - `accent-color` (checkboxes and radios) and `caret-color`: `--color-action`;
  - `::selection`: `--color-action` at 20% opacity under ink text;
  - no sage or browser-default color left anywhere.
- **Type scale (design review 5.1),** Plus Jakarta Sans only, sentence case everywhere, no letter-spaced uppercase labels (this replaces today's uppercase "YOUR SESSION" and "IT IS" labels):

  | Role | Size / line height | Weight |
  |---|---|---|
  | Overlay name | 32/36 | 500 |
  | Intro headline | 30/36 | 500 |
  | Section | 20/26 | 600 |
  | Body | 16/24 | 400 |
  | Chat | 15/22 | 400 |
  | Caption and stats line | 13/18 | 400 (500 for "Verified") |
- **Verified (design review 5.2):** a 16px check-seal icon plus the word "Verified", ink `#171A20`, weight 500, no background or pill (white on photos). Blue never appears on non-actions. Chat: your bubbles in `--color-action`, theirs white with a grey border. Safety strip: light grey.

**Status colors (no green)**
- **Success or done:** ink text with a check icon. No tint.
- **Warning** (guardrail strip): amber icon `#A8741A` with ink text on a light amber tint `#F6ECD9`. Unchanged from today.
- **Error** (form validation, OTP mismatch, feed load error): ink text with an amber warning icon and amber input border. Unchanged from today; no red.
- **Red `#B3402F`** is allowed only on the SOS button, its triggered panel, and the failed-message status text in chat (as the original spec requires).

**Kept as-is**
Gate, login, OTP, verification steps, ground rules, plan, chat guardrails, live session and SOS, companion requests, ops view (palette swap only). Ops stays sans-only and cardless.

**Reuses**
`UnlockImage`, `GateSheet`, `useSession` intent restore (index becomes vertical), `PlanFlow` (adds a topic prefill), `ChatThread`, `HoldToSos`, `LiveOps`.

**2. One brokered session, under a manual safety protocol.** Broker one real, paid 60-minute session in a public café by hand between a companion who said yes and a client.
- **What counts as demand:** the session only counts if the paying client is someone outside your circle of friends. A session with you or your friend is an operational dry run; useful, but not demand.
- **Before the session:**
  - you see both people's government ID (in person or on a video call; seen, not stored);
  - both agree on the café, the time and the 60-minute length;
  - the companion gets your phone number and one escalation contact (yours, or a trusted friend's).
- **During the session:**
  - you stay reachable by phone throughout;
  - the companion messages you on arrival, at the 30-minute mark, and a check-out message once she has left the café, due no later than 10 minutes after the agreed 60-minute end time (a missed check-out starts the escalation ladder at 0 minutes);
  - she can leave at any time and is still paid in full.
- **Escalation ladder** (a check-in or the check-out is late, or she sends any distress message):
  - **0 minutes:** you call her. If she answers and is fine, log it and continue.
  - **5 minutes, no answer:** call again, and call the café to ask staff to check the table. The escalation contact starts heading to the café.
  - **15 minutes, still no contact with her:** call the police (112) with her name, the café address and the client's name, then keep calling her.
  - Write each step down with times. No further sessions run until the incident is reviewed.
- **Numbers:** you hold both phone numbers, so they never exchange them.
- **Payment:** the client pays you by UPI before the session, and you pay the companion by UPI within 24 hours. Record both transactions.

### R3-1 — completeness (blocking)

**Problem**

> The escalation ladder starts when "the check-out is late", but the check-out has no due time. The arrival and 30-minute check-ins are tied to the agreed start time. The check-out is only "when she has left the café". With no deadline, a missing check-out is never "late", so the ladder may never start for the end of the session. **Remedy**

> Give the check-out a deadline, for example no later than 10 minutes after the agreed 60-minute end. Say that missing it starts the ladder at 0 minutes.

### R3-2 — completeness (minor)

**Problem**

> When the trigger was a distress message, "if she answers and is fine, continue" lets a call under duress end the escalation.
