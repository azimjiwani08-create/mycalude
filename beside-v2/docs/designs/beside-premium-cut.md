## Problem Statement
The founder asked what is extra in the current glass redesign, asked for the filter chips (Today, Walks, Coffee, Coaches) to go, and asked how to make Beside feel more premium and grow as a business that works for everyone. Mid-session the founder set two rules in their own words: "It shouldn't be seen as an escort app, so do not rate the price or something", and price must still work because "it should be profitable to girls also".

The audit of the live home screen (logged out, 375px wide) found ten things that add noise or read as a listing of women:
1. Filter chips that sort only 5 companions.
2. Three stacked bottom bars (price bar, 5-tab nav, dev badge) covering about a quarter of the screen.
3. Price shown twice per card, including "₹1,800/hr" on her photo.
4. A "Verified" badge on every card.
5. "Photo unlocks when you verify" repeated on every card.
6. Star ratings on women.
7. A Save button before sign-in.
8. A profile button in both the header and the nav.
9. Sessions and Chat tabs shown to logged-out visitors, where they are dead ends.
10. A strip of cut-off content showing above the sticky header (a bug).

## Demand Evidence
None yet. The founder has shown the prototype to friends only. That is useful for flow bugs, not evidence that companions will list or clients will pay. The previous Assignment (3 companion interviews, 1 brokered paid session) has not been done.

## Status Quo
Unchanged from the prior doc: target members use dating apps partly to practise talking to women. Companions today have no structured, safe, paid way to offer conversation time.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **Member:** the founder or a close friend, first-hand pain, still a hypothesis.
- **Companion:** a communication coach, theatre performer or writer who would set her own fee.
- **Wedge:** one hand-brokered, paid 60-minute session in a public cafe. "Introduce me" is that wedge expressed as a button.

## Constraints
- Everything in the prior doc's Constraints still applies: soft photo files before verification, masked chat, SOS hold, one primary action per screen, 44px targets, no horizontal scroll at 360px, reduced motion, banned words and mechanics.
- New, from the founder: nothing on screen may read as an escort listing, and the economics must clearly favour the companion.

## Premises (agreed)
1. No price and no ratings ever sit next to a woman's photo, in the feed or on the profile hero. Star ratings on people are removed entirely. Trust is stated once: "ID-verified people. Public venues. Safety team on call."
2. Price first appears when a member requests a session, as a session fee for 60 minutes in a named venue, never as "per hour" on a person. Companions set their own fee within a band Beside sets. The band has a minimum and a maximum, so companions cannot be undercut below the floor.
3. Companions keep most of the fee (working assumption 75-80%), are paid if the member no-shows or she leaves early, and see their take-home before accepting.
4. Premium means fewer things, said once. The ten audit items are cut or fixed. One primary action per screen.
5. These cuts are cheap and reversible, so they ship now. Whether companions list and clients pay is still decided by the interviews and one paid session.

## Approaches Considered
- **A. Quiet feed.** Cut the extras, remove stars, move the fee to the request screen. Smallest change, but still a scrollable list of women.
- **B. Introduced, not browsed.** Replace the feed with "tell us what you want to practise, we introduce you". Strongest positioning, biggest rebuild, and with 5 companions the matching is the founder by hand.
- **C. Quiet feed plus "Introduce me" (chosen).** Ships A's cuts and tests B with one entry point.

## Recommended Approach: C
Chosen because it ships every cut now and turns the hand-brokered wedge into a button, so the product and the Assignment test the same thing: do members prefer choosing, or being introduced?

Wireframe (rough, two screens): `/Users/azim/.gstack/projects/besides/designs/mockup-20261029/premium-cut-sketch.png`. AI mockups did not run: the design tool had no OpenAI key, 0 of 3 images saved.

### Screen 1: Home, logged out
Top to bottom:
1. Header: the Beside wordmark and one account button. No chips.
2. Invitation card: "What would you like to practise?", "Tell us, and we'll introduce you to one companion.", and the one primary button **Introduce me**.
3. Label "Or meet this week's companions", then one full-bleed card at a time: soft photo, serif name, role, one "Ask me about" line, and the voice hello below.
4. Once, under the first card: "Photos unlock when you verify".
5. Once, at the end: "ID-verified people. Public venues. Safety team on call."

Removed: filter chips and their filtering code, the price chip, star ratings and session counts on cards, the per-card Verified badge, the per-card unlock pill, Save on cards before sign-in, the fixed price/request bar, and the floating nav for logged-out visitors.

After verification the home keeps the same layout. Save returns on cards, and the floating nav appears with 3 tabs: Home, Sessions, Chat. Profile moves to the header button only.

### Screen 2: "Introduce me" (new, small)
- One question with 3 to 5 tappable answers (for example: speaking up at work, small talk, a first conversation in English, confidence with new people) plus a free-text line.
- The primary button is "Send to Beside". The confirmation reads "We'll introduce you to one companion within a day."
- In the prototype this writes the request to session state and shows it in the ops view. A human (the founder) picks the companion.
- **Pending answer before sign-in:**
  - The button opens the existing verify gate.
  - The answer, including the free-text line, is held in the existing `beside:session` sessionStorage record as `introRequest`, next to the feed's `intent`. It never goes in a URL or a query string.
  - It is sent and cleared as soon as verification completes.
  - If verification is abandoned or fails, nothing is sent. The answer lives only for that browser tab and is gone when the tab closes.
  - An E2E test covers it: answer, verify, the request appears in ops; the URL never contains the free text.
- **After the request (the hand-off to money and consent):**
  1. The founder picks a companion in the ops view.
  2. The companion sees the request in her requests view, with her take-home first, and accepts or declines. The member is told nothing until she accepts.
  3. On acceptance, the member's Sessions tab shows "Meera would like to meet you" with one button, **See the session**. It opens Screen 4 in its introduced mode, pre-filled with her venue and time. The member sees the fee for the first time there.
     - The charge line reads "Meera has already said yes. You're charged when you confirm."
     - The button is **Confirm and pay**. Pressing it charges the member and books the session. She does not accept a second time.
  4. **If the member doesn't confirm:** her acceptance and slot are held until the earlier of 24 hours or 3 hours before the session start. After that cut-off, Confirm and pay is unavailable and the screen reads "This time has passed. We'll find you another."
     - If the confirmed session is under 24 hours away, the charge line adds "This session is inside the 24-hour window, so it can't be refunded if you cancel."
     - The member can decline with a secondary text link under the button, "Not this time". Leaving the screen without pressing either counts as not confirming.
     - If the member declines, or the hold lapses, the hold is released.
     - She is told "The member didn't confirm. Your slot is free again." She is not paid, since no session time was used.
     - Member-facing Sessions states after that:
       - An explicit "Not this time" closes the request at once. Sessions shows "Request closed. Start a new one any time." There is no re-offer.
       - After a lapse, Sessions shows "Finding someone else" while the founder decides.
       - The founder either offers the request to one other companion, which repeats steps 2 to 4 once, or closes it.
       - Closing, or a second lapse, shows "Request closed. Start a new one any time."
  5. If no companion accepts within 24 hours, Sessions shows "No one is free this week. We'll keep looking." with a link to browse companions. Nothing is charged.

### Screen 3: Profile
- The hero photo carries no price and no rating.
- The fixed price bar becomes a single **Request a session** button.
- The rating line is removed. "How safety works" and the availability strip stay.

### Screen 4: Request a session (plan screen)
- This is the first place money appears. Under "What's included" it lists the 60-minute conversation, the venue, the time and "Safety team on call".
- **No photo on this screen.** Her name appears as text in the title, "Your session with Meera". No portrait or thumbnail sits on the same screen as the fee.
- "Session fee" shows the fee with the line "Set by {name}".
- Below it, in the browse path (member picked her): "Most of this goes to {name}. You're only charged once she accepts." The button is **Send request**, and the charge happens when she accepts.
- In the introduced path (she already accepted): "Most of this goes to {name}. {name} has already said yes. You're charged when you confirm." The button is **Confirm and pay**, and the charge happens on that press.
- Then one short terms line, the member-facing version of the rules below: "Free cancellation up to 24 hours before. If she can't make it, you're refunded in full."
- One primary button, per the path above.

### Session terms (both sides see the same rules)
| Situation | Companion | Member |
|---|---|---|
| Member cancels 24+ hours before | Not paid; slot freed | Full refund |
| Member cancels under 24 hours, or doesn't show | Paid in full | Not refunded |
| Companion cancels or doesn't show | Not paid | Full refund |
| Companion leaves early for safety or the member's conduct (flagged by SOS, a check-in or a report to the safety team) | Paid in full | Not refunded; the safety team reviews |
| Companion leaves early for any other reason | Paid for the minutes spent, pro rata | Refunded the unused share of the fee |

Beside never funds a refund from its own pocket: in every row, what the member is refunded is exactly what the companion is not paid, plus or minus Beside's matching share. Her right to leave at any time stays absolute, as in the prior doc's safety protocol; what changes is only how a non-safety early exit is paid.

The companion requests view states her column. The plan screen states the member's short version, and links to this full table.

### Companion side (requests view)
- Each incoming request shows the take-home amount first, for example "You'll receive ₹1,400". Beside's share is shown second.
- The companion's fee is set in her profile settings, within the band.
  - Prototype band: ₹1,200 minimum, ₹3,000 maximum (placeholders).
  - A fee outside the band is not saved. The field shows "Fees run from ₹1,200 to ₹3,000" and keeps the previous value.
- The view states her column of the session terms below.

### Data changes
- `Companion.rating` and `Companion.sessions` stop rendering anywhere member-facing. Keep them in data for ops only, or delete them.
- `pricePlaceholder` is renamed `sessionFee`. It is the default fee.
- A companion's edited fee is stored in session state as `companionFees[companionId]`, like the Introduce me request.
- One helper, `feeFor(companion, session)`, returns the edited fee or the default. The plan screen, the take-home line and the requests view all read it, and nothing else shows a fee.
- `Companion.tags` and the filter logic are deleted with the chips.

## How this grows the business and works for everyone
- **Companions (the hard side):** they aren't ranked or priced on their photo, they set their own fee, they keep most of it, and they're paid for no-shows. That makes them more willing to list, and supply is the bottleneck.
- **Members:** a calmer screen with one clear action and an honest fee shown with what's included, at the moment of commitment. "Introduce me" removes the awkwardness of choosing a woman from a list.
- **Beside:** a lower escort-app perception means less app-store, payment-provider and press risk. Revenue is the platform share (20-25%) of each session. "Introduce me" lets Beside steer demand to available companions, which raises utilisation. Each completed request also shows what members want to practise, which should shape which companions to recruit.
- **Growth loop to test:** a completed session leads to a rebook with the same companion, and the companion's own audience refers members to her Beside page. The second half is untested and is an open question.

### R3-1 — completeness (blocking)

**Problem**

> The introduced-path hold is a flat 24 hours regardless of when the pre-filled session is. If the session she accepted is less than 24 hours away (or a few hours after acceptance), the hold can run past the session start, so the member could press Confirm and pay for a session that has already begun or passed, and the companion keeps a slot reserved up to the meeting time without knowing whether to go. Confirming inside 24 hours of the session also lands the member immediately in the no-refund window of the terms table, which the introduced charge line does not mention.

**Remedy**

> Bound the hold, for example to the earlier of 24 hours or a fixed cut-off before the session start (say 3 hours), state that Confirm and pay is unavailable after that cut-off, and say how the terms line reads when the confirmed session is under 24 hours away.

### R3-2 — completeness (blocking)

**Problem**

> After the first introduced offer lapses or the member declines at the fee, the member's Sessions state is undefined: what she sees while the founder decides, while a second companion is being asked, and what happens if the founder chooses not to re-offer (the only defined closing path is a second lapse). It is also unclear whether a member who explicitly declined at the fee is re-offered at all, and Screen 4 defines only one primary button, so how the member declines (versus simply not confirming) is not specified.

**Remedy**

Start a new one any time." when the founder does not re-offer or the second offer lapses), say whether an explicit decline closes the request immediately, and state how the member declines on Screen 4 (a secondary text link, or leaving the screen counts as not confirming).
